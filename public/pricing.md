# Pricing — Keystone Nurseries

## Free
- Price: Free (no account required)
- No registration or login needed
- Full access to all features

### What's included:
- Nursery directory search by ZIP code, state, or province
- Native plant database with growing conditions (sun, moisture, hardiness zones, height)
- Ecoregion guides with keystone species rankings
- Keystone species reference with caterpillar and pollinator data
- Plant spacing calculator
- Plant quiz to find the right natives for your yard
- State-by-state nursery listings (50 US states)
- Canadian province nursery listings (13 provinces and territories)
- Blog and field guides
